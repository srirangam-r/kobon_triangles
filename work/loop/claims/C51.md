# C51 — splitting a 4-fold point: T′ = T + 2 − (#triangles in a 4-sector "gaining set")

**Lemma (proven, straight lines or pseudolines).**
- *Setting.* P is a 4-fold point with rays in circular order a+, b+, c+, d+, a−, b−, c−, d−,
  and sectors s0 = (a+,b+), …, s7 = (d−,a+).
- *Move.* Translate d by a small ε toward the side of a−, b−, c−. Then d crosses a−, b−, c−
  near P, and P becomes a triple point (a,b,c). No other multiple point or parallel appears.
- *Face changes:*
  - **2 new triangles:** (P, d∩a−, d∩b−) and (P, d∩b−, d∩c−);
  - **4 sectors gain one edge:** s2 = (c+,d+), s4, s5, s7 = (d−,a+);
  - **4 sectors are unchanged:** s0, s1, s3, s6. Their vertex P is replaced by a single
    vertex (P itself, d∩a− or d∩c−).
- So **T′ = T + 2 − t(G)**, with G = {s2, s4, s5, s7}. Moving d the other way gives the
  complement G′ = {s3, s6, s0, s1}.
- *Proof.* Check each face, as in C35. In coordinates: a at 0°, b at 45°, c at 90°, d at 135°,
  and d moved to y = −x − ε. The remainders of s4 and s5 gain the segment of d. s2 and s7
  gain the tiny segments [P, d∩a−] and [P, d∩c−]. s3 and s6 only swap P for d∩a− and d∩c−.
- For a general k-fold point this pattern gives k − 2 new triangles and k gaining sectors
  (k = 3 is C35).

**Corollary (proven).**
- For each line through P, its two gaining sets partition the 8 sectors. So some gaining set
  has ≤ ⌊t(P)/2⌋ triangles.
- **If t(P) ≤ 5, P can be replaced by a triple point without losing triangles.**
- Iterating (the moves are local and monotone), any 94 whose 4-fold points each have ≤ 5
  triangles yields a 94 with triple points only, and k′ = #triple + #4-fold of them.
- With C25: if k′ ≤ 4, there is no 94.

**Residue: "rich" 4-fold points,** with every gaining set holding ≥ 3 triangles. These have
t(P) ≥ 6.
- With exactly 6 triangles, the two empty sectors must be opposite, i.e. never together in a
  gaining set.
- Then ≥ 4 rays are doubly used. With D_P ≤ 4, having 7 or 8 triangles forces ≥ 2 or ≥ 4
  bridges to other multiple points.

**Status.** The lemma is proven by the local analysis; it is not tested (no 4-fold samples).
