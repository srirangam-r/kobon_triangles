**Claim.** No 94 at n = 18 has k = 6, β = 0 and sub-pattern (B).

**Proof.**
- By C28, the 4 singleton ends split into two pairs, each at cyclic distance exactly 5.
  In shape (i) the pairs are the two path-end pairs. In shape (ii) they are the path ends
  and the edge Q–Q′.
- The collinear sub-cases are impossible by C28 Claim 2.
- By C16 + C17 the singleton set must be admissible (`work/t3/wedges.py` rules: even gaps,
  and the wedge directions of every line with two wedged ends are opposite).
- Exhaustive enumeration over all 4-subsets of the 36 end positions:
  - 576 admissible sets (450 with positions 0 and 18 excluded, matching the solver split);
  - **none** of them admits a perfect matching into two pairs at cyclic distance ≤ 5.
- So (B) has no realisation. ∎

singleton sets, which the solver is running.
