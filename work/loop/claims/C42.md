# C42 — k = 5, β > 0: face third vertices and isolated-X credit; 21 centroid-free multisets remain

**(a) Proven.** In an all-multiple face PQR with P and R of type X (F points), the third
vertex Q is **O1b** (one block, capped by a triple line). It cannot be O0.
- Q's triangles include the face and the outer triangles on [P,Q] and [R,Q], which lie in
  sectors (q5,q0), (q0,q1) and (q1,q2).
- C35 needs one more triangle in (q2,q3) or (q4,q5). Either makes q2 or q5 doubly used with a
  simple far end (X_RQ or X_PQ), i.e. a block. So Q has exactly one block, on b_Q.
- By C24 F3, that block is capped by b_P or b_R, which is triple.
- Also: Q's block is mutual with exactly one of P and R. The other F vertex's side toward Q
  is killed only through its second apex. That is the next target, and I have not proven it.

**(b) Proven.** A plain type-X point S whose three lines carry no other triple point
(*isolated*) gives +1 beyond its Lemma-A term.
- Either its axis is a valid L4 axis and claims, or its axis caps some block.
- In the second case the pair (Q, a_S) is a new x pair: every already counted cap passes
  through a triple point other than S, and isolation excludes a_S from being such a cap.
- A non-isolated plain X point lies on a shared line and raises σ. Two such points can share
  one unit of σ.
- So the master condition becomes: for some i ≤ #X,
  2β − (σ_min + ⌈(#X − i)/2⌉) ≥ 2V + 2F + 3O1 + 6O0 + X + O1b + i − 9.
