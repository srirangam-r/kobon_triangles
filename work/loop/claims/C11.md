# C11 — Lemma D: parity through triple points (non-axis lines claim)

**Statement.** Setting: n even, no 4-fold point on the line L. Suppose:
- (i) L is not a cap line: no piece of another line at a simple crossing of L is doubly used;
- (ii) at each triple point P on L, both t-entries of L next to P are a single triangle
  side ("+" or "−", not 0 and not "both"). In particular P is not an end vertex of L.

Let j be the number of triple points on L, and q the number of them where the two entries
next to P have the same sign. **If j + q is even, then L touches an unused bounded segment at
one of its simple crossings** (κ(L) ≥ 1).

**Proof (Blanc's stretch lemma run along all of L).**
- L has r = n−1−j vertices, so there are n−2−j entries between the virtual zeros t_0 and t_r.
- Assume no touch at any simple crossing. Then:
  - zeros are isolated: (0,0) at a simple crossing is a touch, and zeros never sit next to a
    triple point, by (ii);
  - inside a run, the signs alternate at simple crossings, by (i) and step (d). At a triple
    point they alternate or repeat;
  - every run end is next to a zero at a simple crossing, so it is one-sided and has the
    common sign σ.
- So a run with q_r repeats has length ≡ 1 + q_r (mod 2).
- Hence n−2−j = Σ lengths + (#runs − 1) ≡ q + 1 (mod 2). Since n is even, j + q is odd.
  Contradiction. ∎

**Where it applies automatically (proven).** Suppose every triple point on L has two disjoint
blocks, none has L as its axis, and none lies in an all-multiple triangular face. Then L's
entries at P are single and opposite, so q = 0:
- the cap triangles on L's two rays lie in vertically opposite sectors;
- the non-cap sectors are empty by Lemma B.

So **such a non-cap line carrying an even number of triple points claims.**

**Kills.** C12: the stalled k = 4 pattern. More generally it gives one credit per shared
line with an even number of non-axis points. This feeds R2 and the k = 5, 6 patterns.

**SAT form.** For each line L, add auxiliary variables:
- nc(L) := ¬cap-role(L). Take cap-role(L) as the OR over triples t ∌ L of
  z[t] ∧ ≥2 of tri(L,·,·) on t (existing ("cap",L,t) semantics, made two-sided).
- For each triple t ∋ L with t = {L,b,c}:
  - ok(L,t) := z[t] → (each of L's two segments at the point t has exactly one triangle,
    i.e. exactly one of the tri(L,x,y) with x ∈ {b,c} and A(L,x,y) or A(L,y,x) consecutive
    on L at that segment);
  - rep(L,t) := the two triangles lie on the same side of L (via vertex_side of x∩y
    through chi).
- Constraint: nc(L) ∧ ∧_t ok(L,t) ∧ XOR_{t∋L} (z[t] ∧ ¬rep(L,t)) = 0 → OR_{M,d} U(L,M,d).

The XOR says there is an even number of alternating triple points, which is the same as
j + q even. Existing U(L,M,d) clauses give the claim semantics. A cheaper, sufficient special
case to encode first: L carries exactly two triple points, both ok and alternating, and L is
not a cap → claim.

**Empirical.** 113,822 lines satisfying the hypotheses with j + q even: all claim, 0 failures.
92,160 further lines have j + q odd (gallery plus mutated, n = 10–22; `work/t3/lemmaD.py`).
