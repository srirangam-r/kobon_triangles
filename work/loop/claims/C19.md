# C19 — at a bent point, bridges never close a triangle (kills the C08 "−6" configuration)

**Statement.** Setting: P is bent toward Q. Label P's rays 0..5 with middles 1 and 3, shared
ray 2 toward Q, caps C1 ∋ V0, X1, Q and C2 ∋ Q, X3, V4. Let [P,R] be another doubly used
bridge at P (R ≠ Q triple). Then:
- (a) R ∈ {V0, V4, V5}, where V5 is the first vertex of the back ray;
- (b) Q and R are consecutive on no line, so [Q,R] is not a bridge.

In particular the bridge graph has no triangle through a bent point.

**Proof.**
- *(a)* Rays 1 and 3 have simple far ends (block middles), and ray 2 ends at Q. So R is
  the first vertex of ray 0, 4 or 5.
- *(b)* Q's lines are PQ, C1 and C2.
  - **R = V0.** R lies on line(0,3) and on C1. On C1 the vertices V0, X1, Q are
    consecutive: the cap triangles have sides [V0,X1] and [X1,Q] on C1, and these are
    edges. So X1 lies strictly between R and Q on C1.
    - R is not on PQ: PQ ∩ line(0,3) = P, and R ≠ P.
    - R is not on C2: C2 ∩ C1 = Q.
    - So C1 is the only line through both Q and R, and they are not consecutive on it.
  - **R = V4.** The same argument applies with C2 and X3.
  - **R = V5.** R lies on PQ beyond P, so P lies between Q and R on PQ. R is not on C1 or
    C2, because each of those meets PQ only at Q. ∎

**Kills.** The configuration left open in verdicts/C08.md is impossible:
- P bent toward Q with a further bridge to R ∈ {V0, V4, V5};
- Q a one-block point with bridges to both P and R;
- together with S (Φ ≥ 3 − 6 − 1 + 0 − 1 − 1 = −6).

The step that fails is that Q has no bridge to R. So β ≤ 2 among {P,Q,R}, and the crude count
becomes Φ ≥ 3 − 4 − 1 + 0 + m_R + m_S ≥ −5, since m_R ≥ −2 and m_S ≥ −1. More generally, for
k = 4 every triangle in the bridge graph is an all-multiple face with no bent vertex (C4
note (b) and Lemma B).

**SAT form.** Bentness uses blocks sharing a side ray. For triples t_P = {a,b,c} and
t_Q = {a,d,e}, with P, Q consecutive on a, and P's caps d and e:
- bent(P→Q) := z[t_P] ∧ z[t_Q] ∧ tri(a,b,d) ∧ tri(b,c,d) ∧ tri(a,c,e) ∧ tri(b,c,e)
  (the four cap triangles, with the appropriate b/c assignment).
- Clause: bent(P→Q) ∧ dblbridge(P,R) ⇒ ¬cons(Q,R),
- where cons(Q,R) := OR over lines L through both of "no line crosses L strictly between
  them" (A-type adjacency on L), and dblbridge is the two-sided triangle condition on the
  segment [P,R].

The cheaper, sufficient consequence to encode: β(k=4) ≤ 2 whenever a bent point exists.

**Empirical.** 47 bent points with extra bridges (gallery plus mutations, n = 10–18): Q and
R are never consecutive (`work/t3/c20test.py`).
