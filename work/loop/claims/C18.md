# C18 — k = 5, B = 9 (Z = 0, β = 0): carry-over of C16

**Setting.** From C14: a 94 with 5 triple points, β = 0 and B = 9 has:
- Z = 0;
- four type-X points, all killed;
- one 1-block point R, which is an end vertex of the line of one of its non-block rays
  (r2, r3 or r4 in C14's labelling).

**Claim (proven, same arguments as C16).**
- (a) Every line end is simple, except the end(s) at R. A simple end is one of:
  - a mutual end (a wedge, C17);
  - a cap-point end, at a type-X point that is 2nd on its axis;
  - the cap-point end of R's block, when R is 2nd on its block line. In that case t_1 is
    "both".
- (b) Every line that is nobody's axis, does not contain R's block middle, and does not end
  at R, has j_L + ρ_L odd. Proof as C16(i): both ends are wedges, all entries are single,
  repeats occur exactly at cap roles, and entries alternate at type-X points.
  - At R itself entries need not alternate. So lines through R are also excluded unless
    R's entries on them are single and opposite.
- (c) Counting line ends: 36 = 2·#wedges + #(cap-point ends) + #(ends at R), with at least
  one end at R.
- (d) Every non-triple line avoids R and is a cap line, so its two ends are wedges and (b)
  gives ρ odd, i.e. ≥ 1. The count then runs as follows.
  - *Blocks available to non-triple lines.* Mutual blocks are capped by axes, which are
    triple lines. There are 3 + σ non-triple lines and 9 − 2μ non-mutual blocks (R's block
    included). So σ + 2μ ≤ 6.
  - *μ ≤ σ.* A mutual pair is a consecutive pair on a shared line (C15). Such pairs number
    at most Σ_L(j_L − 1) = σ. So 3μ ≤ 6 and μ ≤ 2.
  - *Half-mutual sides.* A killed side of a type-X point P either lies in a mutual pair, or
    is "half-mutual" with R: its apex is R, and R's only block is capped by a_P. At most
    one side is half-mutual, since R has one block with one cap. Write h ≤ 1 for their
    number.
  - *All four type-X points are killed.* Each needs a killed side, so 2μ + h ≥ 4. With
    h ≤ 1 this gives μ = 2, hence σ ≤ 2 and σ ≥ μ = 2, so σ = 2.
  - *h = 0.* Now 5 non-triple lines must cap, one each at least, the 5 non-mutual blocks.
    If h = 1, R's block is capped by the triple line a_P, which leaves only 4 blocks for 5
    lines. So h = 0.
- **Conclusion (proven).**
  - σ = 2 and μ = 2, and the two mutual pairs cover all four type-X points, one side each.
  - Every type-X point is the 2nd vertex of its axis (its other side is a ray).
  - The 5 non-mutual blocks (four type-X blocks and R's block) are capped by the 5
    non-triple lines, one each.

**Status.** Proven by hand (not refereed). Proposed.

**SAT form.**
- σ = 2 means exactly two "extra" incidences: Σ_L max(0, #z-triples on L − 1) = 2.
- Exactly 5 lines avoid all z-triples, each in exactly one ("cap",L,t) role.
- C17 end-pairing clauses.
- The parity XOR for lines in no axis role and not through R.

**Empirical.** None (these would be 94s).
