# C45 — two more rules for the k = 5, β > 0 residue: bent-gap σ, and bent partners cannot supply C44 (ii)

**(a) Bent-gap σ (proven).**
- *Setting.* Let V be bent toward Q, with an extra bridge to W. By C19 (a), W = V0 ∈ C1 or
  W = V4 ∈ C2, and both caps pass through Q. On that cap the vertices W, X, Q are consecutive,
  with X simple.
- *Consequence.* So W and Q are adjacent triple points on a shared line but not consecutive.
- σ = Σ_L (#adjacent triple pairs on L), so each such pair adds 1 beyond the consecutive-pair
  count σ_min.
- Distinct pairs (W,Q) are distinct adjacent pairs.

**(b) Proven.**
- *Setting.* In a 2-F face PQR (O1b vertex Q, C42), suppose Q is the partner of a bent V.
- By C08/C40, Q's block middle lies on line VQ beyond Q. By C42 it lies on b_Q. So **VQ = b_Q**,
  and V's caps are m_PQ and m_QR.
- The caps meet Q with neighbours X1 on m_PQ and X3 on m_QR. Both are simple, and they lie
  opposite P and R (face sides are edges).
- So Q's sector (m_PQ beyond Q, m_QR beyond Q) has simple first vertices X1 and X3. A triangle
  there would need ℓ1 = ℓ3, which is false.
- Hence **C44 (ii) is impossible**, and C44 (i) must hold: P or R has a second face.
