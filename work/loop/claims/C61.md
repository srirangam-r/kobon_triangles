# C61 — the mutual-apex exception at O1b points, and the 124 all-credit survivors at K = 6

## (a) Case lemma (proven)

**Setting.** Let Q be O1b and not the third vertex of a 2-F face. Its block has middle r0 on ℓ, cap C ∋ V5, X, V1, and a triple apex T ∈ {V1, V5}. Suppose C50's exception (i) holds: T has a block whose middle is T→X (along C), capped by ℓ.

**Geometry.**
- The triangle QXT is a cap triangle of both Q and T. So at T the ray T→Q is next to T→X, and it is a side ray of T's block.
- T's cap ℓ meets T's other side ray at its first vertex W. On ℓ the vertices W, X, Q are consecutive.

**Cases by the type of T.**
- **T of type F.** Each block of T covers a face-partner ray (C24 F1). So either:
  - **(α)** Q is a face partner of T (Q lies in a face with T); or
  - **(β)** W is T's face partner. Then W and Q are adjacent triple points on ℓ, not consecutive (X lies between). That is an uncounted gap, so σ + 1.
- **T bent** (partner P′). T's block at T→X is next to the shared ray.
  - "Shared ray = T→Q" would make Q the partner of T. Then Q's block middle would lie on TQ, not on ℓ, so this is impossible.
  - Hence W = P′, and (Q, P′) is a gap on ℓ: σ + 1. The exception is when [T,Q] is a bridge, in which case (Q, P′) is already the C45(a) bent gap.
- **T a centroid.** The same as bent, with W the arm point.
- **T plain X.** The pair (T, ℓ) is T's kill pair, which is already counted as T's +1. So Q's lost credit is not double-counted.
- **T of type O1.** (Q, T) is a **mutual 1-block pair**.
- **T of type O0.** It has no block, so (i) is impossible.

**Consequence.** Q's C50 credit (+1) is lost only in one of these situations:
- a face containing Q and an F apex;
- a σ + 1 gap (W on ℓ next to Q);
- a plain-X apex whose kill pair is (T, ℓ);
- a mutual O1 pair;
- the line-end exception (ii).

This is not yet encoded in the filter.

## (b) The 124 all-credit survivors (K = 6, with C35)

They survive even if every C43 and C50 credit is granted.

- **By multiset:**
  - (F,F,F,F,O1b,O1b): 60
  - (F,F,F,V,O1b,O1b): 18
  - (F,F,V,V,O1b,O1b): 9
  - 6 each: (X,F,F,F,O1b,O1b), (F,F,F,F,O1b,O0), (F,F,F,O1b,O1b,O1b)
  - a tail of smaller multisets
- **Common features:**
  - they are F-heavy (≥ 2 F points; 110 have no plain X);
  - they have 2–3 faces and β = 7–8;
  - their slack is 2–4.
- **Credit split:**
  - C50 credits are few (0–3), because most O1b points are 2-F-face vertices and hence mutual.
  - C43 face credits are 0–2 and are absorbed by the slack. The largest class is c50 = 0, 2F = 2, slack 2, with 30 graphs.
- **Needed:** a new per-F-point credit. Every one of the 124 has ≥ 4 F-type points, or F points plus bent points. Candidates:
  - a parity or claim on F points' axes, which are killed on both sides by mutual pairs (as in the k = 5 t = 8 zigzag);
  - a Hall bound (C38) on the whole face cluster.
