# C10 — Lemma A (cap touch) as a local clause

**Statement.** Let P = a∩b∩c be a triple point and C ∉ {a,b,c} a line with tri(a,b,C) and
tri(a,c,C). Then:
- P's ray along a toward X = a∩C is a block middle with cap line C, and [P,X] is doubly used.
- For every further line N ∉ {a,b,c,C}: if tri(a,C,N) holds, then C∩N = C∩b or C∩N = C∩c,
  that is, z(b,C,N) or z(c,C,N).
- Consequently, if C∩b and C∩c are simple points, the segment of a beyond X is unused
  (when bounded) and C touches it at X.

**Proof.**
- **The cap triangles.** The triangles (a,b,C) and (a,c,C) both have the side [P,X] on a,
  on opposite sides of a. Their sides on C are [C∩b, X] and [X, C∩c]. These are edges,
  since no line enters a face. So C∩b and C∩c are the two neighbours of X on C.
- **Where the extra triangle sits.** The triangle (a,C,N) has X as a vertex, and a∩N ≠ P
  because N ∉ {b,c}. So its side on a starts at X and goes away from P. Its side on C is an
  edge of C at X, so its other endpoint C∩N is a neighbour of X on C, which gives
  z(b,C,N) or z(c,C,N).
- **The consequence.** The faces over the segment of a beyond X are exactly the faces
  bounded by a (beyond X) and by C's edges at X. By the above, neither is a triangle
  unless one of those neighbours is a triple point.
- Everything used holds for pseudolines. ∎

**Mutual pair.** When z(b,C,N) holds, the point Q = b∩C∩N has the doubly used first segment
[Q,X] (on C), and its cap line is a.

**Kills.** This supplies the Lemma A credit used in C4 and C5 and in the k ≥ 4 accounting.
As a clause it forbids "triangle beyond a cap point" unless there is a triple apex.

**SAT form (build_defect variables).** For all distinct a,b,c,C,N with b < c:

  (¬z[abc] ∨ ¬tri[abC] ∨ ¬tri[acC] ∨ ¬tri[aCN] ∨ z[bCN] ∨ z[cCN])

Triples are sorted in the index. This is about 816·3·15·14 ≈ 5·10^5 clauses of 6 literals.
Optional strengthening, touch form, if U-type variables exist for non-clean lines: the same
premises with ¬z[bCN'] and ¬z[cCN'] for all N' imply that the piece of a beyond X is unused.

**Empirical.** 30,210 instances of the premises (gallery plus mutated arrangements,
n = 10–22), 0 violations (`work/t3/lemmaD.py`).
