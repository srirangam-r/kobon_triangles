# C55–C59 — the centroid at k = 6

Script: `work/t3/kN_fixed2.py K c35|noc35`. It is the referee's p11_fixed generalised to K points, plus the verdict-C54 fixes and the rules below. It reproduces the 32 rows at K = 5.

**C55 (proven): side gaps.**
- A centroid's three arm partners are pairwise adjacent on the triangle sides, but not consecutive: a simple cevian foot lies between them.
- So σ ≥ σ_min + 3, by C40's structure.

**C56 (proven): O1b neighbour.**
- An O1b point q has a consecutive triple neighbour T on a side ray of its block.
- T is never an arm-giver: bent and centroid arms lie on the ray opposite q's block middle.
- If q has no bridge or face partner other than its arm-givers, (q,T) is an uncounted consecutive pair.
- Such pairs add ≥ ⌈n/2⌉ to σ, since two O1b points may be each other's T.

**C57 (proven): centroid structure.** A partner Q's consecutive triple neighbours are only P, its two side extensions, and (only if Q is O0) its cevian-beyond. Hence:
- arm partners are pairwise not bridged;
- each partner has ≤ 2 non-arm bridges (≤ 3 if O0);
- any other point has ≤ 1 bridge to the partners of one centroid, since it lies on at most one side line.

**C58 (proven): C44(ii).** C44(ii) cannot be supplied by a centroid arm, because the arm lies on q's block line (the cevian), not on m_QR.

**Result.** At k = 6 only one centroid graph survives, in both modes: (X, X, C, O1a, O1a, O1a), with bridges only the 3 arms and slack 0.

**C59 (proven): that graph is impossible.**
- **Credits.** Each O1a arm partner Q_i earns a C50 Lemma-A touch at its cap point Y_i (on the cevian beyond Q_i). The only exception is Y_i being the cevian's end; mutual apexes are impossible for O1a. Slack 0 then forces all three Q_i to be 2nd on their cevians.
- **Arcs.** The rays r0, r2, r4 from P, toward Q0, Q2, Q4, then carry only the vertices Q_i and Y_i. They cut the circle at infinity into three arcs, and each sector has angle < π.
- **Counting.** By the sector lemma (C27, applied at P), the arc between the ends of ℓ0 and ℓ2 holds only the ends of ℓ1 (P's third line) and of C5, C3, C_{Q0}, C_{Q2}. That is ≤ 5 ends, and the same holds for each arc.
- **Contradiction.** The other 33 ends would need to fit in ≤ 15 places. ∎

**Consequence.** **No centroid in a 94 at k = 6**, given the refereed ingredients and C55–C59. Residue at k = 6, β > 0, centroid-free:
- with C35: 1,910 graphs;
- without C35: 2,084 graphs.
