# C46 — a segment borders at most two faces (applied to the k = 5 residue)

**Fact (trivial, proven).** A consecutive pair of triple points spans one segment, which
borders exactly two faces. So at most two all-multiple faces contain a given pair.

**Use.** Several filter survivors had three bridge-graph triangles sharing a side, for example
{0,3,4}, {1,3,4} and {2,3,4}. Since every bridge-graph triangle is a face (pairwise consecutive
points form a face), these are impossible.

**Result.** Referee's corrected filter `work/referee4/p10_fixed.py` plus C46, i.e.
`work/t3/k5_p10fixed_c46.py cent`: **12 centroid-free multisets / 38 graphs** (was 13 / 44).
Per-multiset counts are in `work/t3/k5_p10c46.out`.
