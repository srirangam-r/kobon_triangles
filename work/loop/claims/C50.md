# C50 — Lemma-A touch for 1-block points; an O1b point in two 2-F faces is impossible

**(a) Lemma (proven, C10 applied to the block).**
- *Setting.* Let Q have one block, with middle r0 on line ℓ, cap point X and cap C.
- *Claim.* The segment of ℓ beyond X is unused and touched by C at X, which is a Cred +1
  distinct from all counted credits, unless one of these holds:
  - (i) an apex V ∈ {first vertex of r1, first vertex of r5} is triple and has a block toward X
    capped by ℓ (a mutual apex);
  - (ii) X is an end vertex of ℓ.
- For O1a points (i) is impossible, since their apexes are simple.
- For the O1b vertex of a 2-F face, (i) always holds (C42).

**(b) Proven.** At k = 5, an O1b point Q cannot be the third vertex of two 2-F faces.
- Each face occupies a sector at Q flanked by its two outer triangles, which are the F points'
  cap triangles. The two faces then fill all 6 sectors.
- So the two remaining rays are doubly used.
- Each is a block (so Q has 2 blocks, contradicting O1b) or a bridge. A bridge would need a
  fifth distinct neighbour, and Q already has four.
- This kills the three (F,F,F,F,O1b) graphs.
