# C62 — twin faces: two all-multiple faces on one side [a,b] cannot have crossed blocks at their third vertices

**Lemma (proven, lines or pseudolines, any k).**
- *Setting.* Δ1 = (P1, a, b) and Δ2 = (P2, a, b) are all-multiple triangular faces with the common side
  [a, b], so they lie on opposite sides of it.
- *Definition.* A block of P1 *covers* P1→a if its middle is the neighbour of the ray P1→a other than P1→b.
- *Claim.* P1 has no block covering P1→a while P2 has a block covering P2→b (and likewise with a, b swapped).
- *Corollary.* P1 and P2 are not both type X. By C24 F1 (k-independent: the middles of a type-X face vertex
  are its third line), a type-X third vertex has blocks covering both of its face rays.

*Proof.*
1. At a, Δ1 and Δ2 fill the two sectors next to a→b. So a→P1, a→b, a→P2 are consecutive, a's lines are
   m_{aP1}, m_{ab}, m_{aP2}, and the rays at a in circular order are a→P1, a→b, a→P2, a→−P1, a→−b, a→−P2.
   The same holds at b.
2. Circular order at P1: P1→a, P1→b, ρ′, P1→−a, P1→−b, ρ. The block covering P1→a has middle ρ and covers
   P1→−b, ρ, P1→a.
   - Its cap triangle in the sector (ρ, P1→a) has vertex a. At a it lies in the sector next to a→P1 other
     than Δ1, which is (a→−P2, a→P1).
   - So the block's far end is X1 = first(a→−P2), the cap is m_{aP2}, and X1 lies on m_{aP2} beyond a, on
     the side away from P2.
3. The second cap triangle lies in (P1→−b, ρ), with apex M1 = first(P1→−b) ∈ m_{bP1}. The two apexes are
   the two neighbours of the cap point on the cap (C10). So M1 is X1's neighbour on m_{aP2} other than a:
   M1 = m_{bP1} ∩ m_{aP2}, and the order on m_{aP2} is P2, a, X1, M1.
4. Apply steps 2 and 3 to P2's block covering P2→b, swapping P1 ↔ P2 and a ↔ b. Its cap is m_{bP1}, and its
   second apex is M2 = first(P2→−a) = m_{aP2} ∩ m_{bP1}. M2 lies on the ray of m_{aP2} from P2 away from a.
5. m_{aP2} and m_{bP1} cross once, so M1 = M2. But M1 is beyond a on the far side from P2, and M2 is beyond
   P2 on the far side from a. Contradiction. ∎

(In straight-line terms: P1's block needs m_{aP2} and m_{bP1} to converge on P1's side, while P2's block needs
them to converge on P2's side.)
