# C49 — an F point with two faces adds two σ gaps

**Claim (proven).**
- *Setting.* Let P be type X with all-multiple faces in both non-cap sectors, with vertices
  A1, A2 (first vertices of r1, r2) and A4, A5.
- P's caps pass through first vertices: C0 ∋ A5, X0, A1 and C3 ∋ A2, X3, A4. Both triples are
  consecutive on the cap, with X0 and X3 simple.
- *Claim.* So (A1, A5) and (A2, A4) are adjacent, non-consecutive triple pairs on C0 and C3.
  Each adds 1 to σ beyond the consecutive-pair count.
- They are not bridges or face sides, and in these graphs they are not bent gaps.
