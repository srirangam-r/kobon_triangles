# C26 verdict: rigidity of singleton ends in C16 (A)/(B)

**Verdict: refereed-correct.** All four parts were re-derived.

## Logic

**(a)**
- A singleton end is not a wedge. With Z = 0 and no triple end vertex, t_1 is "both"
  (C16/C17).
- By L1, X_2 is multiple. Since β = 0, l_1 is a block middle of X_2, and since X_2 is type
  X (C14), ℓ is its axis.
- tri(ℓ,C,b) and tri(ℓ,C,c) are the two cap triangles at X_1. ✓

**(b)**
- X_3 is P's other cap point, which is simple, capped by D.
- Its far side [X_3, X_4] is bounded, and P is killed (C14), so this side is mutual.
- By C10 and C15, V_s = Q lies on b or c, consecutive with P, and [Q, X_3] is Q's block
  middle with cap ℓ. Since Q is type X, D = a_Q.
- The face PQX_3 (lines ℓ, D, m) is a cap triangle of both P and Q.
- Q's two cap triangles at X_3 have lines D, ℓ and each of Q's other lines. ✓

**(c) Case (A).**
- Every point has exactly one mutual side. Each mutual block is in exactly one pair (C16
  addendum: a double cross needs σ ≥ 7). So the mutual pairs are a perfect matching of the
  6 points, and hence of the 6 singletons.
  - The singletons correspond one-to-one to the points: each point is 2nd from exactly one
    end of its axis.
- For a matched pair, X = a_P ∩ a_Q is the mutual cap point of both, i.e. X_3 from each
  singleton end.
- Matched singletons lie on different lines: two points on one axis cannot also be
  consecutive on a second line. ✓

**(d) Case (B).**
- The degrees in the mutual graph are 1 for the four singleton points and 2 for the other
  two, over 4 edges. Multi-edges are impossible: one pair of points gives at most one X.
- If the two degree-2 vertices are adjacent, the graph is P–A–B–P′ plus Q–Q′.
- Otherwise it is two cherries, P–A–P′ and Q–B–Q′.
- There are no cycles, because degree-1 vertices exist and two vertices cannot form a
  cycle. ✓

## SAT forms

These are all sound, since each is a necessary condition:
- "exactly 3 lines cross ℓ strictly before ℓ′ from e": X_1 contributes 1 line and P
  contributes 2;
- the 15 matchings;
- "singleton lines are exactly the axis lines";
- the tri() block and face literals.
