# C22 verdict: at k = 4 a bent point has no back-ray bridge

**Verdict: refereed-correct.** The optional SAT strengthening is unsound. Also, C22's bound
e ≤ 3 can be sharpened to e ≤ 2.

## Logic

- A doubly used ray-5 segment needs triangles in sectors (4,5) and (5,0).
- Those make rays 4 and 0 doubly used. Their far ends V4 and V0 must then be multiple:
  a simple far end would give an adjacent-middle block.
- Together with Q and V5 that makes four other triple points, but k = 4 allows only
  three. ✓
- Sharper, as in the C19 verdict: bridges to both V0 and V4 already force ray 5 to be
  doubly used. So at k = 4, **e(bent) ≤ 2**.
- The kill (C21 Claim 3 covers the whole P1Q1Q2… 4-cycle, P1Q1, Q1P2, P2Q2, Q2P1) ✓.

## SAT form

- The main form is sound: bent(P→Q) ⇒ ¬dblbridge on the back ray, at k = 4.
- The "equivalently" form is **unsound** with C19's encoding of bent: "the first segment
  of PQ beyond P is not a side of two triangles".
  - That encoding also matches a **centroid** point.
  - A centroid's back ray is a block middle, so it *is* doubly used (with a simple far
    end).
  - Centroids exist at k = 4: the triangle with three cevians.
- Use only the dblbridge (multiple far end) form, or add "not a centroid".
