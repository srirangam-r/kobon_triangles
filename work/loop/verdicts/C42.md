## (b) Isolated X credit

- An isolated plain X point S has no mutual partner, so it is unkilled and gives A ≥ 1.
- a_S carries only S, so either L4 applies or a_S caps some block of Q.
  - L4 applies: both Lemma-A sides are unkilled, or S is 2nd on its axis (C13 form).
  - In the capping case, (Q, a_S) is a new pair. Every counted cap passes through a triple
    point T ≠ S: a partner, a face vertex, a mutual partner, or the triple first vertex of
    an O1b point's side ray. Each of these would share a line with S, so a_S can be none of
    them.
  - These pairs are distinct for distinct S.
- A non-isolated plain X point S has no bridge and lies in no face. So its consecutive
  pair on a shared line is neither a bridge nor a face side, which means it is not in
  σ_min. One pair covers at most 2 such points, giving ⌈(#X − i)/2⌉. ✓
- The "∃ i" form of the filter is sound.
