# C63 verdict: the fan lemma

**Verdict: refereed-correct.** The lemma holds at any k; the corollary holds at k = 5. It kills graphs 20–25, all six
(F,F,O1b,O1b,O1b) graphs.

## Proof check (re-derived)

- **Setting.** ρ0..ρ3 have triple first vertices, so a block middle (which needs a simple far end) can only be ρ4 or ρ5.
- **Block at ρ4.**
  - (ρ3,ρ4) and (ρ4,ρ5) are its cap triangles, and (ρ5,ρ0) is a triangle because ρ0 is doubly used. So ρ5 is doubly used.
  - A simple far end for ρ5 would give adjacent block middles, which L2 forbids (C07 verdict):
    - with distinct caps, the two caps would share the far ends of ρ4 and ρ5;
    - with equal caps, one cap would cross ρ3 and ρ0, the two opposite rays of one line.
  - So ρ5 is a bridge. ✓
- **Block at ρ5.** Symmetric. ✓
- **Corollary.**
  - Consecutive faces at Q share the sides Q→b and Q→c, so ρ1 and ρ2 are doubly used with triple ends. This gives
    e(Q) ≥ 4.
  - A block would force a fifth bridge. That needs a fifth distinct neighbour, since two rays to one point would be two
    lines through two points. At k = 5 there is none. ✓
- **Inputs.** The only input is L2 (refereed). Nothing depends on C35 or on any proposed claim.
