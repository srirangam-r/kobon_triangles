With β > 0 the two C23/C24 hypotheses are complementary. ✓

## Must not be encoded as written

These variants are **unsound**:
- the C19 "cheap" consequence β(k=4) ≤ 2 whenever a bent point exists;
- the C20(a) SAT form without "r3 not doubly used";
- the C22 "equivalently" strengthening, which a centroid violates;
- the C23 "β ≤ 2 when v = 1 and some X point exists", applied without the no-X-face
  hypothesis;
- C19's "no bridge triangle through a bent point", applied at k ≥ 5.

C25 itself uses none of them.
