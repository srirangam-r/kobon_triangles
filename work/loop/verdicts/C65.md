# C65 verdict: converging caps (no all-multiple face has two type-X vertices)

**Verdict: refereed-correct.** The lemma is right, for lines and pseudolines and at any k. It kills graphs 0, 2–11, 13, 15
and 17. A computer check that I built independently proves it exhaustively for pseudolines (see "Evidence").

## Proof check (re-derived)

- **Step 1.** Take the face PQR.
  - At R the rays R→P, R→Q and γ are consecutive, so they lie on three distinct lines. Hence γ lies on b_R.
  - Look at the cap triangle R–X–Q. At Q it fills the sector next to Q→R, on the side away from P. The bounding ray of
    that sector belongs to b_Q (rays at a triple point alternate between the three lines). So the cap is b_Q.
  - The second cap triangle is R–X–Y, with Y = first(R→−P). The side X–Y lies on the cap, because X is simple.
  - So b_Q meets m_PR beyond R. ✓
- **Step 2.** The same argument at P puts b_Q ∩ m_PR beyond P. ✓
- **Step 3.** b_Q ≠ m_PR, the two lines cross once, and the rays of m_PR beyond P and beyond R are disjoint. This is a
  contradiction. ✓ (If b_Q ∥ m_PR, neither block exists.)
- **Corollary.** C24 F1 is k-independent: at a type-X face vertex the middles lie on b_P, so the vertex covers both of its
  face rays. So two type-X vertices P and R both cover toward Q, which is impossible. ✓

## Consistency with earlier refereed work

- **Nothing proven is contradicted.**
  - The referee3 proof of C24 F2 ("b_Q meets line PR beyond P and beyond R", in the C24 and C34 verdicts) uses only the two
    X vertices P and R, so it already proves C65.
  - C24 F3 (case x_f = 2), C42 (a), C43/E1/E2, C44, C45 (b), C48 type A and C50 (b) are all conditional on a 2-F face. They
    become vacuous; none of them becomes wrong.
  - Removing 2-F faces can only kill residue graphs. It cannot revive any.
- **Nothing observed is contradicted.** No 2-F face was ever seen: the C24, C25 and C34 verdicts report "no sampled face
  had two X vertices", and my gallery runs (below) find none either.

## Evidence (referee scripts in work/referee4/c62_65/, structure code struct_.py written independently)

- **Deletion argument.** The premise (a face PQR plus blocks of P and R covering toward Q) is positive. It lives on the six
  lines through P, Q and R, since both caps are Q-lines.
  - Triple points, faces and blocks survive deleting the other lines. They also survive adding a far pseudoline.
  - So a counterexample at any n gives one on exactly 6 lines.
- **Exhaustive enumeration (enum.py).** It enumerates every rank-3 signotope with zeros and no 4-fold point, and checks
  the reproduced counts of simple ones: 62 at n = 5 and 908 at n = 6.
  - n = 6: 4,848 signotopes, 56 all-multiple faces, 48 of them with one X vertex, 0 with two. "Two vertices cover toward
    the third" never occurs; "one vertex covers" occurs 168 times.
  - n = 7: 253,108 signotopes, 7,336 all-multiple faces, 0 violations.
  - Together with the deletion argument, this is a complete proof for pseudolines.
- **Premise SAT with any number of triple points (premise_sat.py).**
  - The premise is UNSAT at n = 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
  - The control (one covering block, all-multiple face) is SAT, and the decoded model was re-checked.
- **Gallery:** all 10,326 arrangements; 76 all-multiple faces, 9 with one X vertex, 0 with two.
- **Mutations** (gallery_test.py mutate 1500 11 4: contractions, flips and greedy premise-seeking contractions):
  36,024 arrangements, 40,777 all-multiple faces, 949 with one X vertex, 0 with two. Covering toward a vertex occurs
  once 31,797 times and twice never.

## The generator's evidence
