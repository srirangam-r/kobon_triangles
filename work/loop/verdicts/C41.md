**Bridges.** P's only bridges are its 3 arms, since r1, r3, r5 are middles with simple far
ends.
- Q_i and Q_j share only the cap line, with the simple point X between them, so they are

**σ ≥ 6 + e(R).**

- A bridge R–Q_i puts R on one of Q_i's lines, all of which are among the six.

  cevian, R lies beyond Q_i.
- So 2β − σ ≤ e(R). ✓
