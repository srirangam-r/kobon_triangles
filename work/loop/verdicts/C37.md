# C37 verdict: k = 5, β ≥ 1 profile filter (88 multisets / 2,505 bridge graphs)

**Verdict: refereed-correct.** Every exclusion rule in `work/t3/k5_profiles.py` is a valid
necessary condition, so the filter never drops a real configuration. Re-running it gives
88 / 2,505.

## Rules checked

**Degree sets.**
- X {0}, F {2,4} (C34), V {1,2,4} (C34), C {3}.
- O1 {1..4} and O0 {2..4}: C35 gives the lower bounds, and e ≤ 4 because bridges from one
  point go to distinct other triple points.

**Graph structure.**
- The graph is K4-free (Lemma K4).
- Every G-triangle is a face: three pairwise-consecutive triple points bound a face, as in
  the K4 argument.
- An F point with e = 2 forms a face with its two neighbours: its two bridges bound one
  non-cap sector.
- Faces satisfy F2 and F3 (k-independent, C34): no face has 3 X/F vertices; with 2 of them
  the third is O1 or O0.
- An X-in-face is automatically F, since both face sides are doubly used (F1).
- Leniencies: bent points in faces are allowed, and so are F4 points and the C35 r3
  refinement. These only err toward keeping configurations.

**Bent partners.**
- Every V point has its partner as a G-neighbour.
- A partner takes at most 1 V point if it is O1, and 2 if it is O0 (C08, C21).
- The C19 (b) non-adjacency condition is applied.

**Counts.**
- B + β ≥ 9 is the D-count.
- The master inequality is exact algebra. Per-point bounds: X ≥ −2, F ≥ −1, V = −1,
  C = −3, O1 ≥ 0, O0 = 3. Substituting #C = 5 − (others) gives exactly
  2β − σ ≥ 2#V + 2#F + 3#O1 + 6#O0 + #X − 9.
- Replacing σ by its lower bound β + #(non-bridge face sides) only weakens the rule.

## Note

The survivors are an over-approximation. As C37 says, they are not a case split usable to
exclude solver cases beyond those listed. The recommendation to add C36 §3–§4 to k5b is
sound (C36 verdict).
