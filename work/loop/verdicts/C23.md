## Lemma K4

- All 6 segments are edges, so they form a non-crossing drawing of K4. One point, P4, lies
  inside the triangle P1P2P3.
- The line P1P4 continues beyond P4. It cannot cross lines P1P2 or P1P3 again.
- If it passed through P2, then P4 would lie on line P1P2. But that line meets the closed
  triangle only in the edge [P1,P2], so P4 would not be interior.
- So it crosses [P2,P3] in its interior, and P2, P3 are not consecutive. ✓ This holds for
  pseudolines too.
- Corollary: three pairwise-consecutive triple points bound a face, because no line can
  enter the triangle.
