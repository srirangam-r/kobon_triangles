## The O0 face-vertex issue (no-C35 mode)

The script applies two rules to every third vertex q of a 2-F face:
- the C44 (ii) rule "a triangle in (q3,q4) ⟹ q has another bridge";
- the C45 (b) rule "a bent partner cannot supply (ii)".

Both need q to have its block with middle q5:
- For C44 (ii), a triangle in (q3,q4) needs (q4,q5) to be a triangle too.
- For C45 (b), the argument needs VQ = b_Q.

Without C35, q may be **O0**, and then both rules fail. **Fix:** in no-C35 mode, apply
these rules only when q is O1b.
