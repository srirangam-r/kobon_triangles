# C45 verdict: bent-gap σ, bent partners in 2-F faces, the k5_profiles10 filter

**Verdict: refereed-fixed.** (a) is correct only for W ∈ {V0, V4}, and the filter
over-counts at a degree-4 bent point. (b) is correct.

## (a) Bent-gap σ

- For W = V0 or V4: W, X, Q are consecutive on a cap line with X simple. So (W, Q) is a
  consecutive triple-point pair on that line. It is neither a bridge nor a face side, and
  it adds 1 to σ beyond σ_min. ✓
- **Gap in the claim: at k = 5 a bent point can have e = 4** (extra set {0,4,5}, using
  Q, V0, V4, V5 all triple). The back-ray neighbour V5 lies on line VQ with V between V5 and
  Q. Its pairs (V5,V) and (V,Q) are bridges, which σ_min already counts, so (V5,Q) is **not**
  a gap pair.
- `k5_profiles10.py` counts every non-partner neighbour of a bent point as a gap, so it
  over-counts σ_min and the filter is unsound there.
- **Fix:** at a degree-4 bent point, count only 2 gaps, minimised over which neighbour is V5.

## (b) Bent partners cannot supply C44 (ii)

- The C08/C40 fact puts Q's middle on VQ, and C42 puts it on b_Q. So VQ = b_Q, and V's caps
  are m_PQ and m_QR.
- V's simple block far ends X1 and X3 are Q's neighbours on those lines, opposite P and R.
- So a triangle in (m_PQ beyond Q, m_QR beyond Q) is impossible: a doubly used ray with a
  simple far end would be a block adjacent to Q's middle. Hence (ii) fails and (i) must
  hold. ✓
