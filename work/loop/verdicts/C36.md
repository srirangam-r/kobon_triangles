    cyclic order alternates lines. So the two triangles on [P,Q] are tri(r,x,y) and
    tri(r,x′,y′) under one of the two pairings.

- **§3 (Σu − Σblk − Σbr ≤ −9).**

    assignment Σu = Z_simple ≤ Z.
  - A true blk implies a doubly used segment with a simple far end. The two triangles
    share the Q-line C, and bridge triangles cannot. So a true blk is a real block, and
    each block counts once.
  - A true br implies a real bridge, and at most one pairing holds per bridge.
  - In the real assignment Σblk = B and Σbr = β, and Z_simple ≤ Z ≤ B + β − 9. Sound. ✓
