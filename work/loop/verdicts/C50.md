# C50 verdict: 1-block Lemma-A touch; an O1b point cannot be in two 2-F faces

**Verdict: refereed-correct.**

## (a) The 1-block Lemma-A touch

- A triangle beyond the cap point X needs a triple apex, the first vertex of r1 or r5,
  with a block toward X capped by ℓ. That is exception (i), by C10. Otherwise the segment
  of ℓ beyond X is unused and touched by C, unless it is a ray, which is exception (ii).
- An unkilled cap point belongs to exactly one block. C is a cap line, so this touch is
  distinct from C11, C13, shared-axis, clean and C43 touches.
- (i) is impossible for O1a points.
- (i) always holds for the O1b vertex of a 2-F face: P is the apex, with its block at X_PQ
  capped by b_Q. ✓

## (b) An O1b point in two 2-F faces is impossible

- The second face cannot lie in s2 or s4. Its outer ray would start at the simple point
  X_RQ or X_PQ.
- So it lies in s3, and all 6 sectors are triangles.
- Then q2 is doubly used. With a simple far end that gives a 2nd block, which
  contradicts O1b. With a triple far end it needs a 6th triple point.
- The 3 (F,F,F,F,O1b) graphs each have two G-triangle faces through the O1b point, so they
  die. ✓
