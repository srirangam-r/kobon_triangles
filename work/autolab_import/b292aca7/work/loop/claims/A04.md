# A04 — Interrupted triple gaps and the high-line-count residue

**Status: proposed, not self-refereed.** One round-2 proof task: narrow t=7,8 without splitting. Work in the pairwise-intersecting affine setting of C34, with 18 lines, exactly t triple points and no higher point. Assume T>=94. Extension to arrangements with parallel lines requires the project's separate normalization argument; it is not proved here.

Let G join consecutive **triple points** along each line, ignoring simple crossings. It is a simple graph with sigma=3t-m edges, where m lines contain triples. Unlike A01's bridge graph, G need not be plane: its edges may contain simple crossings. Let H be the doubly used bridge subgraph, beta=|E(H)|. Let h count edges of G not in H. Thus h=sigma-beta exactly. Use B=sum b(P), C=#{b(P)=3}, Z=#unused bounded segments.

## 1. Centroids spend interrupted gaps

A block's cap has consecutive vertices Q,X,R, with X simple (C10, A02). When both outer vertices Q,R are triple, QR is an edge of G but not H. Call it an interrupted cap gap. Let J count these gaps, and q count block-cap incidences with both outer vertices triple. Then

    3C <= q <= 2J <= 2h.

**Proof.** At a centroid the three non-middle rays are bridges (C07), so each of its three distinct caps contributes such an incidence. A gap Q-X-R has a unique internal simple point X. There is just one other arrangement line through X; a block with this cap and middle endpoint X must have its apex at the first vertex on one of that line's two rays from X. Thus at most two blocks charge the gap. This counts cap incidences, not x-credits; nothing is added to the master inequality's per-point m-values.

Consequently, with d=2t+C-B=2n_0+n_1>=0,

    d + h - C + Z <= 2t+6-m,
    h >= ceil(3C/2),
    m + Z + ceil(C/2) <= 2t+6.                 (GAP)

These follow by substituting beta=3t-m-h in B+beta>=3t-6+Z. They cover centroids, unlike the old C=0 window. In particular Z<=20-m at t=7 and Z<=22-m at t=8.

A centroid and its three bridge neighbours give six distinct edges of G: its three spokes and its three interrupted cap gaps. Thus **C>0 implies sigma>=6**. If sigma=6, these exhaust G. Every neighbour has at most one block: viewed at that neighbour, the two triangles on its spoke force any block middle to lie on the continuation of the spoke away from the centroid (the same local argument as C34(c)'s bent-partner fact). Thus the four points contribute at most 5/2-3/2=1 to sum c(P); all other points have e=0 and b<=2, hence c<=0. Therefore **sigma=6 with a centroid cannot support T>=94 for t>=8**, since C34 requires sum c>=t-6+Z>=2.

## 2. Seven triples on all eighteen lines are impossible

Here sigma=3, so C=0. Put d=14-B=2n_0+n_1. C34 says

    beta-d >= 1+Z,   beta<=3,   d+Z<=2.        (7)

A point isolated in G with two blocks is axis type, not bent. It cannot have a mutual partner (such a partner would be consecutive on a shared line). Lemma A therefore gives at least one touch at that point. These touches are distinct (C10/general.md), so I isolated axis points force **2Z>=I**. This is not the unproved assertion that every line with two triples yields an unused segment; C11 has extra hypotheses.

There are two cases.

* **G contains a cycle.** Its three edges must form a triangle, with four isolated vertices. At least one bridge is needed by (7). If all three triangle vertices had b=2, none could be bent (a bent partner has b<=1). They would all be axis points. An axis bridge forces the all-multiple face on those same three vertices (Lemma B), but a face cannot have three axis vertices (C34(b), C24 F2). Thus the triangle has total b<=5, so B<=13, d>=1 and Z<=1. Also B>=12+Z, so its four isolated vertices have total b>=7+Z. At least three are axis points. Hence 2Z>=3, contradicting Z<=1.

* **G is a forest.** Axis points have no bridges, since Lemma B would give a triangle in G. Each bent point has exactly one bridge, to its partner: an extra bridge to V0 or V4 creates the cap gap Q-X-V0 or Q-X-V4 and hence a triangle in G; a back-ray bridge forces both outer bridges by the corrected C07/C19 local sector argument. Each bent point therefore is a leaf of H. A partner has b<=1, receives at most two bent leaves, and if two then b=0 (C34(c)). All other nonisolated vertices of H have b<=1. By (7), d<=2. If d<=1, there is at most one low-block vertex, at most one bent leaf, and beta<=1<=d. If d=2 with one blockless vertex, beta<=2=d. Both fail (7). The only remaining possibility is two 1-block vertices, each receiving at most one bent leaf, and possibly an edge between them. To have beta-d>=1 requires all three edges: H=G is the four-vertex path bent--one-block--one-block--bent, and Z=0. The other three points are isolated axis points (the deficit d is exhausted), forcing 2Z>=3, again impossible.

Thus **t=7 implies m<=17**, in this setting, without assuming any six-point closure.

## 3. Explicit remaining windows

For t=7, m=16 or 17 has sigma<=5 and hence C=0. With d=14-B:

| t | m | sigma | residue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 17 | 4 | d+Z<=3; d+1+Z<=beta<=4 |
| 7 | 16 | 5 | d+Z<=4; d+1+Z<=beta<=5 |
| 8 | 18 | 6 | C=0 by section 1; d=16-B, d+Z<=4; d+2+Z<=beta<=6 |

In each row 0<=n_0<=floor(d/2), n_1=d-2n_0, n_2=t-n_0-n_1, all nonnegative. This completely lists the **aggregate** profiles, not the geometric configurations. `work/t3/a_gap.py --profiles` emits every row as JSONL. For the remaining m (7..15 at t=7, 8..17 at t=8), retain centroids and use (GAP); no closure is claimed. The ranges follow 3t<=binom(m,2), a necessary incidence condition.

Tests: `python3 work/t3/a_gap.py --selftest 200` checks the gap incidence/injection on synthetic exact arrangements and exhausts a finite abstract relaxation of the sigma=3 graph argument. Result: 93 eligible arrangements, 110 skipped because of higher multiplicity, 35 cap incidences, one explicit centroid, and 6,576 abstract states, all passing. The three-edge shape list is exhaustive: either a triangle, a connected tree (star/path), a two-edge path plus disjoint edge, or a matching; isolated vertices are restored to total seven. Results are in `work/t3/a_gap_validation.json`. Tests are diagnostics, not a proof certificate or a new construction score. SAT implementation details and remaining cases are in A05.
